# [M4] Show the health score, budget, spending and data freshness on Home

## 📖 Story / Why

Home tells someone what their month did. M4 makes it say **whether that is good**: a Money
Health Score they can watch move, how the month sits against its budget, where the money
went, and how current all of it is (PRD F6).

People don't read dashboards, they glance at them. So the score has to be readable at a
glance and explainable in one tap. And the "as of" date has to be visible, because until
bank linking, every number is only as fresh as the last statement imported.

## 🧭 Context

- Repo: `FinAI-Mobile-2026`. **No brief.** Drafted from `docs/ROADMAP.md` (4.4) and PRD
  **F6**, **F8** and **F12**.
- **Builds on the redesigned Home in PR #45** (`import-results-and-live-dashboard`), which
  must be merged first. It contains:
  - The green field: hero, day-by-day chart, four figure cards, actions.
  - The sheet: recent transactions, commitments.
  - The collapsing header and the bottom bar (Home, Transactions, Review).
  - Shared components to reuse:
    - Android: `ui/components/GreenField.kt` (`Field`, `Waves`, `FieldVectors`),
      `ui/components/CategoryVisuals.kt` (`CategoryIcon.vector()`, `.tint(dark)`),
      `ui/components/MintScreen.kt`, and `FigureCard`/`EqualGrid` in
      `ui/dashboard/DashboardScreen.kt`.
    - iOS: the matching views in `iosApp/iosApp/ui/DashboardView.swift`,
      `ui/components/CategoryVisuals.swift` and `ui/components/MintComponents.swift`.
- **Server side is 4.3.** `GET /dashboard` gains these fields, all optional and null when off
  or absent:
  - `spend_by_category`
  - `budget` (lines with `allocated`, `spent`, `over`; totals; `shortfall`)
  - `health_score` (`score`, `formula_version`, `scored_on`, `previous_score`)
  - `as_of` (`latest_transaction_on`, `last_import_at`)
  - `learning` (`ready`, progress, needs)

  The score's components are on `GET /health-score` (4.2) and are fetched only when the
  breakdown is opened.
- **The client computes none of these figures** (`CLAUDE.md` → Who computes what). Shared
  code may only fold the response into screen state: ordering, which lines to show, progress
  fractions for bars, labels.
- **Design: none provided. Manager decision, 2026-10-04: "match Home".** Build in the existing
  Home design language: the green field, glass pills, white rounded cards, the category
  tiles and tints from `CategoryVisuals`, and the type scale already used. Don't introduce
  new colours or a new card style.
- **Layout (to follow unless the PM says otherwise at `/start-ticket`):**
  - **In the green field:**
    - The **freshness line** directly under the hero figure ("As of Oct 2 · last import 3
      days ago").
    - The **score card** between the day-by-day chart and the four figure cards: a ring with
      the score out of 100, "+4 since last month", and a chevron.
  - **In the sheet, above "Recent transactions":**
    - **"This month's budget":** the totals and the 4 lines nearest or over their
      allocation, with progress bars. "See budget" opens the Budget tab from **4.5**. Until
      4.5 lands, the link is hidden.
    - **"Where it went":** spending by category, the top 5 with category tiles and amounts,
      plus "Show all" expanding in place.
- **Learning state (PRD F8):** when `learning.ready` is false, the score card and the budget
  section are replaced by **one** "Still learning your patterns" card. It shows progress
  toward the threshold, e.g. "1 of 1 full month · 12 of 20 transactions", and points to
  importing another statement. Spending by category still shows; it needs no history.
- **Gating:** each section is drawn only when the capabilities payload enables it
  (`auto_budget` for the budget, `health_score` for the score) **and** the field is present.
  An older server, or a feature that's off, means the section isn't drawn and no error is
  shown.
- **Hiding amounts:** the eye toggle masks every new figure the way it masks the rest of Home.
  The score is not money and stays visible.
- **Copy:** the score is educational guidance, not advice (`CLAUDE.md` → positioning). The
  breakdown sheet carries the platform disclaimer line, and component copy describes, never
  instructs: "You kept 12 % of your income", not "You should save more".

      - the freshness line

      on Android and iOS, matching each other.

- [ ] A feature disabled in capabilities hides its section even when the field is present.
- [ ] The eye toggle masks every new money figure.
- [ ] **No figure is computed on the client.** Every amount, the score and the change come from
      the response. A review checks the folds only order, select and format.
- [ ] A stale dataset (newest transaction more than 30 days old) shows the freshness line as a
      nudge to import.
- [ ] Every new string is in `i18n`, and there are no literals in platform code.

      The PR states all three.

## 🖼️ UI standards

**No design is provided. Build it against the existing Home design.** The PM may supply
mockups at `/start-ticket`. **Ask before building**; if they exist, match them exactly.

- [ ] **Design system:** reuse Home's tokens and components (`Field`, glass pills, white
      cards, `CategoryVisuals` tiles and tints, the existing type scale). No one-off colours or
      sizes, no duplicated component code.
- [ ] **Light and dark themes:** every colour comes from tokens defined in both modes. Check
      the field, the cards and the breakdown sheet in both.
- [ ] **Native components:** the breakdown sheet is `ModalBottomSheet` on Android and
      `.sheet` on iOS. The progress bars are native (`LinearProgressIndicator` / `ProgressView`)
      unless styling needs otherwise. If the ring can't be native, say so and keep it minimal.
- [ ] **Edge-to-edge and insets:** the new field content respects the status bar and the
      collapsing header. Nothing sits under the bottom bar or the gesture area.
- [ ] **Responsive:** small and large phones, landscape. Content width stays capped at 560 as
      on the rest of Home.
- [ ] **Truncation:** long category names and amounts ellipsize on one line. Amounts never
      clip digits (reuse `FittedAmount`'s approach where a figure is large).
- [ ] **States:**
      - **loading:** the existing coin loader on first paint
      - **refreshing:** figures stay up
      - **learning:** the card above
      - **error:** a failed refresh keeps the last figures and never zeroes them
      - **feature off / field absent:** the section isn't drawn
- [ ] **Accessibility:**
      - The score ring reads "Money Health Score 72 out of 100, up 4 since last month".
      - Each budget line reads category, spent, allocated and whether it's over.
      - Touch targets are at least 48dp / 44pt.
      - Layouts hold at the largest font scale.
      - Colour is never the only cue for "over budget"; there is a label or icon too.
- [ ] **Motion:** any ring or bar animation respects reduce-motion.
- [ ] **i18n:** every string goes through `sharedLogic/i18n`.
- [ ] **Architecture:** native UI per platform, folds in `sharedLogic`, VMs drive rendering
      (`kmp-arch-v2`).
- [ ] **Verify** on the smallest and largest phone, light and dark, and the largest font
      scale, in every state above.
