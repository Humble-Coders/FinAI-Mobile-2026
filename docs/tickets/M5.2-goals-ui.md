# [M5] Goals: set savings targets and see what each one needs

## 📖 Story / Why

The backend (5.1) turns a goal — "$6,000 for a car by next September" — into what it needs
every month and whether the person is on pace. This ticket is where they set those goals,
reorder them by what matters most, add money as they save, and see the plan at a glance:
on a **Goals** tab of their own, and in a short summary on Home (PRD **F5**, **F6**).

Two things must hold:

- **Every figure is the server's.** The monthly amount, the completion month, the progress,
  "on track" — all come from 5.1. The app formats and orders them; it never works one out.
- **Long-term numbers are labelled for what they are.** They carry the platform's
  not-financial-advice disclaimer, and they say plainly that they do not include investment
  growth — because in this version they don't.

## 🧭 Context

- Repo: `FinAI-Mobile-2026`. **No brief** — manager decision, 2026-10-06, as for M3 and M4.
  Drafted from `docs/ROADMAP.md` (5.2) and PRD **F5**, **F6**, **F8**. The Product Owner
  confirms the decisions below at `/review-ticket`.
- **Server side is 5.1** (Finance-backend). Read its ticket for the full contract:
  - `GET /goals` → `goals` (in priority order), `budget` (the comparison, or null with a
    `reason`: `unavailable` / `learning` / `no_savings_line`), `disclaimer_version`,
    `projection_version`, `assumes_growth`.
  - Each goal: `id`, `name`, `kind`, `horizon`, `target`, `saved`, `remaining`, `target_date`,
    `monthly_contribution`, `required_monthly`, `projected_completion` (`YYYY-MM`),
    `progress_percent`, `status` (`achieved` / `overdue` / `on_track` / `behind` / `open`),
    `achieved_at`, `priority`.
  - `POST /goals`, `PATCH /goals/{id}`, `POST /goals/{id}/add {amount}`,
    `PUT /goals/order {ids}`, `DELETE /goals/{id}`, and `GET /legal/disclaimer`.
  - Errors: `403 feature_unavailable`, `404 not_found`, `409 goal_limit_reached`,
    `422 invalid_amount`, `422 date_in_past`, `422 order_mismatch`.
- **Manager decisions, 2026-10-06:**
  - **Goals replaces Review on the bar:** Home · Transactions · Budget · **Goals**. Review is
    already reachable from Home, and stays reachable from the import result and from the Budget
    tab's "transactions aren't filed yet" link — it simply stops being a tab. **It now opens
    over the bar with a back arrow**, like Income and Expenses do, and back returns to the
    screen it was opened from.
  - **Progress is entered by the person** — we never see balances. "Add money" adds to a goal;
    the editor can also set the saved amount outright.
  - **Savings goals only.** No debt-payoff goals.
  - **No investment growth yet.** When `assumes_growth` is false, long-term goals say so in one
    plain line. Growth, and how debts affect long-term goals, are follow-ups on the backend.
  - **The budget comparison is read-only.** "Your goals need $650 a month · your budget sets
    aside $900", or the shortfall. Nothing on the Budget tab changes.
- **Goals are not gated by "still learning".** PRD F8 lets goals work from the first day. Only
  the budget comparison is missing while learning — say so in that one place, not by hiding goals.
- **The tab is gated by the `goals` capability** (seeded by 5.1), exactly as Budget is gated by
  `auto_budget`. **Carry over #47's lesson** (PR #50, three review rounds): *not known yet is
  not the same as off.* After the system reclaims the app, the saved route brings the person
  back to their tab before capabilities have loaded. `tabsFor` / `leavesBudget` in
  `navigation/AppNavigation.kt` and `FeaturesViewModel.showsBudget` / `leavesBudget` on iOS
  already handle that for Budget. **Extend the same rule to Goals** — do not write a second one.
- **Disclaimers come from the server** (PRD §4.6 — the apps cannot be hot-fixed). Fetch
  `GET /legal/disclaimer` and show it wherever a long-term goal's projection is shown. Its
  current text is a draft placeholder; that is a backend follow-up, not this ticket's.
- **Design: none provided. Manager decision: "match Home"**, as for 4.4 and 4.5. Use the green
  field and white sheet, the category-tile style from `CategoryVisuals`, the editor-sheet
  pattern from `ui/edit/TransactionEditor.kt` / `CorrectionSheet`, and the type scale already in
  use. **At `/start-ticket`, ask the PM whether mockups exist after all** — if they do, match
  them exactly.
- **The screen:**
  - **Header (in the field):** "Your goals", and — when there is one — the budget comparison
    line, or the shortfall said plainly.
  - **List (in the sheet), in priority order:** per goal a tile for its `kind`, the name,
    "$1,200 of $6,000", a progress bar from `progress_percent`, and one status line:
    - `on_track` / `behind`: "Needs $334 a month · you plan $250" — **both figures from the
      server; never subtract them**
    - date only: "Needs $334 a month until Sep 2027"
    - contribution only: "Done by Mar 2028 at $250 a month"
    - `open` with neither: "Add a date or a monthly amount to see a plan"
    - `overdue`: the date has passed, said in words; `achieved`: done, said in words
  - Long-term goals add "Doesn't include investment growth" while `assumes_growth` is false,
    and the disclaimer.
  - A "New goal" action. Reordering by priority.
- **Editing:** a sheet in the transaction editor's style — name, kind (tiles), horizon
  (short / long), target, **already saved (optional)**, target date (optional), monthly amount
  (optional). "Add money" is its own small sheet with one amount field. Delete asks to confirm
  first: it cannot be undone.
- **Validation is shared.** `GoalEdit.blockingReason(...)` returns one reason or none; the
  disabled Save, the inline notice and the use case's thrown error all read it (`CLAUDE.md` →
  Blocking reasons). **Reuse `BudgetEdit`'s too-precise check** — move it somewhere both can
  call rather than copying it: `Money.normalize` declines `"500.999"` and `"five hundred"` with
  the same null, and the person deserves to be told which.
- **After a write, tell Home.** Add a `GoalsChanged` signal on both platforms, in the pattern of
  `BudgetChanged` (#47): emitted only after the server confirms, collected by Home's goals card.
- **Home's goals card** (needs **#46** merged): up to three goals in priority order with their
  progress, or an invitation to set one; tapping it selects the Goals tab. Drawn only when the
  `goals` capability is on.
- **Health score:** 5.3 adds a `goal_completion` component to the score. Add its one plain
  sentence to the score breakdown's strings here, so the breakdown (#46) reads it as soon as
  5.3 deploys.

## 🎯 Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] With 5.1 deployed, the bar reads **Home · Transactions · Budget · Goals** (each gated by
      its capability), and Review opens from Home, the import result and the Budget tab's
      unfiled link — with a back arrow that returns to where it was opened from.
- [ ] Creating a goal with a date shows the server's monthly amount; with a monthly amount, its
      completion month; with both, on track or behind, with both figures shown and **neither
      subtracted on the device**.
- [ ] **Add money** raises the goal's saved amount and progress, and a goal reaching its target
      reads as achieved.
- [ ] Reordering sticks across leaving and returning, and Home's card shows the new top three.
- [ ] **Long-term goals** show "Doesn't include investment growth" while `assumes_growth` is
      false, and the server's disclaimer text.
- [ ] The budget comparison appears when the budget is ready, and when learning says why it is
      missing — while **goals themselves keep working**.
- [ ] Save is disabled with the right inline reason for every `GoalBlock`, and the use case
      refuses the same drafts with the same reason, from one shared function.
- [ ] A failed save keeps the sheet open with the person's input and the error; nothing is
      half-applied. The 21st goal shows the limit's own message.
- [ ] Delete asks for confirmation and removes the goal; the list and Home's card update.
- [ ] **Restored onto the Goals tab after the system reclaims the app, the person stays there**
      — they are not sent to Home while capabilities load. Checked on a device with
      `adb shell am kill com.humblesolutions.finai` (not Force stop) and by force-quitting on iOS.
- [ ] With `goals` disabled in capabilities, the tab and Home's card do not appear; a 403 shows
      its own message.
- [ ] No figure is computed on the client.
- [ ] `./gradlew ktlintCheck`, the Android build and tests, and the iOS `xcodebuild` all pass.
      The PR states all three.

## 🖼️ UI standards

**No design is provided — build it against the existing Home and Budget design.** The PM may
supply mockups at `/start-ticket`; **ask before building**, and if they exist, match them exactly.

- [ ] **Design system:** reuse `Field`, `Waves`, `FieldVectors`, the sheet, `CategoryVisuals`-style
      tiles, `LearningCard`'s card style and the editor-sheet pattern. No one-off colours or sizes,
      no copied components.
- [ ] **Light and dark themes:** every colour from tokens in both modes. Check the field, list,
      sheets and Home's card in both.
- [ ] **Native components:** a bottom-bar tab (`NavigationBarItem` / `TabView` item); the
      editors as `ModalBottomSheet` / `.sheet` (iOS `Form`); the date as the platform date picker;
      progress bars native where possible. **Reordering:** iOS has it natively (`List` +
      `.onMove`); Compose has no native list reorder — **tell the PM, then use a minimal
      drag handle plus "Move up" / "Move down" accessibility actions**, which are needed anyway.
- [ ] **Edge-to-edge and insets:** the field runs under the status bar; content clears the
      status bar, the bottom bar and the gesture area; sheets stay above the keyboard.
- [ ] **Responsive:** small and large phones, landscape. Content width capped at 560.
- [ ] **Keyboard:**
      - amounts use the **decimal pad** with the currency symbol, and an on-keyboard **Done**
        (iOS has no return key on the pad)
      - the name field uses words capitalisation; **Next** moves through the editor's fields
      - the focused field and its error stay visible above the keyboard
- [ ] **Truncation:** long goal names ellipsize on one line; amounts never clip.
- [ ] **States:**
      - **loading:** the coin loader on first load; **refreshing:** figures stay up
      - **empty:** an invitation to set a first goal
      - **error:** inline with retry; a failed refresh keeps the last figures
      - **disabled Save:** with its reason; **saving:** busy button, **inputs locked**
- [ ] **Preserve state:** an editor or add-money amount being typed survives rotation and process
      death (Android `SavedStateHandle`, iOS `@SceneStorage`), **including the bind that follows a
      restore** — #47 shipped this broken twice before getting it right.
- [ ] **Accessibility:**
      - each goal reads once, as one sentence: "Car, saved $1,200 of $6,000, needs $334 a month,
        on track"
      - status is never colour alone — it is always in words
      - touch targets at least 48dp / 44pt; layouts hold at the largest font scale
- [ ] **Motion:** bar animations respect reduce-motion.
- [ ] **i18n:** every string through `sharedLogic/i18n`; no literals in platform code.
- [ ] **Architecture:** native UI per platform, decisions and folds in `sharedLogic`, VMs drive
      rendering (`kmp-arch-v2`).
- [ ] **Verify** on the smallest and largest phone, light and dark, and the largest font scale,
      in every state above.
